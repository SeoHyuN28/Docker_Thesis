Says when it isn't sure

Claude is built to flag its own uncertainty rather than paper over it — useful when the cost of a confident wrong answer is higher than a slower right one.

Built to be a careful conversationalist, not just a fast one.

Anthropic designs Claude around a simple bet: an assistant is more useful when it can be questioned, corrected, and reasoned with in the open, rather than treated as a finished oracle.
