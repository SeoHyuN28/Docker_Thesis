About

Made by a safety-focused AI lab

Claude is built by Anthropic, a company founded on the idea that AI systems should be developed carefully, studied closely, and made genuinely useful along the way.

Why Anthropic exists

Anthropic was started by a group of researchers who believed that increasingly capable AI systems needed serious safety work alongside their development — not bolted on afterward. That premise still shapes how Claude is trained, tested, and released today.

What "Claude" refers to

Claude is the name of Anthropic's family of AI models, and also the name of the assistant you talk to when you use one of them. Successive generations have improved at reasoning, coding, writing, and working with longer and more complex material, while keeping the same underlying approach: an assistant that explains itself and can be corrected.

Where you can use it

Claude is available through a chat interface on the web and in mobile apps, through a developer API for building it into other products, and through tools aimed at specific kinds of work — writing code, editing documents, and working inside a spreadsheet, among others.

How it's meant to be used

Claude works best treated as a collaborator you stay in the loop with: give it real context, push back when something's off, and check its work on anything that matters. It's built to support that back-and-forth rather than to be taken as a final word.

A rough timeline

  • 2021Anthropic is founded, with AI safety research as its founding focus.
  • 2023Claude becomes publicly available as a chat assistant and developer API.
  • SinceSuccessive model generations expand what Claude can read, reason about, and help build.
